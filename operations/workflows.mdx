Standardized workflows ensure consistency across the team, reduce errors, and make onboarding new team members faster. When every deal, exit, capital call, and reporting cycle follows the same sequence of steps — with clear ownership at each stage — nothing falls through the cracks and the knowledge base stays current. This page documents the standard processes for the most common operations at Capria.

## New Investment Workflow

Follow these steps every time Capria closes a new investment to ensure the deal is properly recorded, the fund model is updated, and LPs are notified as required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Investment Decision">
    Confirm that IC approval has been obtained and the term sheet has been signed by all parties. Do not proceed to legal execution until both conditions are met.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legal Execution">
    Finalize and execute the subscription agreement and all investment documents. Initiate the wire transfer once all signatures are in place and legal has confirmed the documents are in order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record in Knowledge Base">
    Create the investment record in the knowledge base. Include the fund name, portfolio company, investment amount, instrument type (equity, SAFE, convertible note, etc.), transaction date, pre-money valuation, and resulting ownership percentage on a fully diluted basis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Company Profile">
    Add a new portfolio company profile or update the existing one. Confirm the stage, sector, founding team members, website, and any other material company details are current and accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reserve Allocation">
    Record the follow-on reserve allocation for this investment in the fund model. Document the rationale for the reserve amount and the approval from the investment team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notify LP Administrator">
    Inform the fund administrator of the new investment so they can update the fund's NAV and capital account records. Provide the transaction date, amount, and company details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capital Call (if needed)">
    If capital is being drawn down to fund the investment, initiate the capital call workflow. Issue the capital call notice to LPs per the process documented in the Capital Call Workflow section below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exit Workflow

Follow these steps when Capria realizes a full or partial exit — whether through an M&A transaction, IPO, or secondary sale — to ensure proceeds are accurately calculated, distributed, and recorded.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Exit Event Confirmed">
    Confirm that the exit event is legally closed: M&A closing documents signed, IPO pricing finalized, or secondary sale agreement executed. Do not begin proceeds calculations until the event is legally binding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gross Proceeds Calculated">
    Determine Capria's total share of proceeds from the exit. Deduct any applicable transaction costs, escrow holdbacks, or legal fees to arrive at net proceeds available for distribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Waterfall Calculation">
    Run the distribution waterfall model in accordance with the fund's LPA. Calculate the allocation between LPs (return of capital, preferred return, carried interest) and the GP. Have the CFO review and approve the waterfall output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribution Notice Prepared">
    Draft the distribution notice using the approved waterfall figures. Include each LP's total distribution amount, the breakdown by return of capital versus gains, wire instructions for receiving proceeds, and the expected distribution date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribution Notice Sent">
    Deliver the distribution notice to all LPs via the LP portal and email at least five business days before the distribution date. Confirm receipt and resolve any LP questions before wires are executed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wires Executed">
    Execute outgoing wires to each LP in accordance with their wire instructions on file. Confirm all wires are sent and note any that fail or are returned for correction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Records">
    Update the portfolio company status to **Exited** in the knowledge base. Record the exit date, gross and net proceeds, and DPI impact. Update the fund-level TVPI and DPI calculations to reflect the realized return.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quarterly Reporting Workflow

The quarterly reporting cycle runs on a defined timeline relative to quarter-end (T). Follow these steps to produce accurate, timely LP reports each quarter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data Collection — T+1 to T+15">
    Send data collection requests to all portfolio companies within the first business day after quarter-end. Collect financials, KPIs, and any material company updates. Chase outstanding responses to ensure all data is received by T+15.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Valuation Review — T+15 to T+30">
    Review all portfolio company valuation marks in light of new data, recent comparable transactions, and market conditions. Obtain CFO approval for all marks before they are finalized. Document the methodology and key inputs for each valuation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund-Level Calculations — T+30 to T+45">
    Calculate fund-level performance metrics — TVPI, DPI, and IRR — as of quarter-end using the approved valuation marks and reconciled capital account data. Confirm that these figures are consistent with the fund model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="LP Capital Account Statements — T+40 to T+50">
    Coordinate with the fund administrator to prepare individual LP capital account statements. Reconcile each LP's called capital, distributions received, and ending NAV against knowledge base records. Resolve any discrepancies before the statements are finalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report Drafting — T+45 to T+55">
    Draft the quarterly LP report using approved data. Include portfolio company updates, fund-level performance metrics, a market commentary, and any material fund news. Ensure all figures are labeled as gross or net with the correct as-of date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Internal Review — T+55 to T+60">
    Circulate the draft report for a two-person review (typically the Portfolio team lead and CFO). Incorporate all feedback and corrections before the report is marked ready for distribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribution — T+60">
    Upload the final report and LP capital account statements to the LP portal. Send a notification email to all LPs with a link to the portal. Archive the distributed report in the knowledge base under the correct fund and quarter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capital Call Workflow

Follow these steps whenever Capria draws capital from LPs — whether for a new investment, management fees, or fund expenses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify Capital Need">
    Confirm the reason for the capital call: new investment, management fee draw, fund expenses, or other permitted use under the LPA. Document the total amount required and its purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calculate LP Shares">
    Pro-rate the total capital call amount across all LPs based on each LP's commitment percentage. Confirm that the total does not exceed each LP's remaining uncalled commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft Capital Call Notice">
    Prepare the capital call notice. Include the total call amount, each LP's individual call amount, wire instructions, the due date, and the purpose of the capital call as required by the LPA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legal / CFO Review">
    Submit the draft notice for review by the CFO and legal counsel. Confirm that the call complies with the LPA in terms of notice period, permitted use, and available uncalled capital. Obtain approval before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Notice">
    Deliver the approved capital call notice to all LPs via the LP portal and email. Send the notice 10 to 15 business days before the due date to provide adequate time for LP wire processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Receipts">
    Monitor incoming wires daily as the due date approaches. Update the capital call tracking log as each LP's wire is received. Follow up promptly with any LP whose payment has not arrived by the due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record in Knowledge Base">
    Once all wires are received (or the due date has passed), update the knowledge base to reflect the new called capital, remaining uncalled capital, and current fund cash position for each LP and at the fund level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Team Responsibilities Matrix

The table below summarizes who owns each workflow, who reviews the output, and what LP communication is required.

| Workflow | Primary Owner | Reviewer | LP Communication |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Investment | Deal team | CFO, General Counsel | Capital call (if applicable) |
| Exit / Distribution | CFO | GP, Legal | Distribution notice |
| Quarterly Reporting | Portfolio team + CFO | GP | Quarterly report |
| Capital Call | CFO | GP | Capital call notice |
| Valuation Update | Investment team + CFO | Auditors (annually) | LP report |
