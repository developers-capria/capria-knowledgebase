This guide walks you through the key areas of the Capria Ventures knowledge base so you can find the information you need quickly and keep fund and portfolio data accurate and up to date. Follow the steps below to orient yourself, then explore the linked sections to go deeper into each area.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore Fund Overview">
    Navigate to [Fund Management](/funds/overview) to see all of your active funds, their structure, current lifecycle stage, and capital deployment status. This is your starting point for understanding where each vehicle stands against its investment period targets and committed capital.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Portfolio Companies">
    Visit [Portfolio Management](/portfolio/overview) to find individual company profiles, investment details, ownership records, and monitoring dashboards. Use the portfolio view to get a snapshot of the full portfolio or drill into a specific company to review its latest updates and financials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Performance Metrics">
    Head to [Performance & Reporting](/reporting/metrics) to review IRR, MOIC, and TVPI across funds and the aggregate portfolio. Compare current performance against prior periods and benchmark targets to identify trends and prepare for LP conversations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Reports">
    Use the [LP Reports](/reporting/lp-reports) section to create and distribute investor reports. Select the fund, reporting period, and data points you want to include, then export or share the report directly with LPs through the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>
