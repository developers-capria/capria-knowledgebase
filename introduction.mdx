## What's Inside

The Capria Ventures knowledge base is organized into focused sections that mirror how the investment team operates day to day. Whether you are preparing for an LP meeting, reviewing a portfolio company's latest metrics, or tracking capital deployment against a fund's target, the sections below cover everything you need to know.

<Accordion title="Fund Management">
  The Fund Management section covers the full lifecycle of each fund, from initial structuring through final close and eventual wind-down. You will find tools and documentation for tracking fund structure and terms, managing capital call schedules, recording LP commitments, and modeling distribution waterfalls. Each fund's investment period, harvest period, and key milestones are maintained here so the team always has a clear picture of where each vehicle stands relative to its lifecycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Portfolio Management">
  The Portfolio Management section serves as the central record for every company in which Capria Ventures has made an equity investment. Each portfolio company has a dedicated profile containing investment history, ownership details, follow-on round tracking, and board or observer seat information. The monitoring dashboards surface key operational and financial updates, allowing the investment team to spot risks early and identify opportunities for follow-on investment or strategic support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Performance & Reporting">
  The Performance & Reporting section gives you a comprehensive view of how each fund and the overall portfolio are performing against targets and benchmarks. You can review standard VC metrics — IRR, MOIC, TVPI, DPI, and RVPI — at the fund level and rolled up across the firm. The LP reporting tools allow you to generate periodic investor reports, customize data views by fund or cohort, and benchmark performance against relevant public market equivalents and peer funds.
</Accordion>
