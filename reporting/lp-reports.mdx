LP reports are the primary communication channel between Capria and its limited partners. They are not merely a compliance obligation — well-prepared reports build trust, demonstrate transparency, and give LPs the information they need to understand how their capital is being managed. Every report reflects Capria's professionalism and commitment to the highest standards of investor relations. Understand what each report must contain, when it must be delivered, and how it reaches LPs so that every communication meets the obligations set out in the LPA and the expectations Capria has set with its investor base.

## Report Types

Capria issues several types of reports and notices to LPs throughout the fund lifecycle. Each serves a distinct purpose and has its own content requirements and delivery timeline.

| Report Type | Frequency | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly Report | Quarterly | Fund metrics, portfolio updates, capital account statements |
| Annual Report | Annually | Audited financials, full portfolio review, fund performance, management letter |
| Capital Call Notice | As needed | Call amount, purpose, wire instructions, due date |
| Distribution Notice | As needed | Distribution amount, source, LP allocation |
| Special Update | As needed | Major portfolio event (large exit, down round, company failure) |

## Quarterly Report Structure

Each quarterly LP report follows a standard structure. Consistency across reporting periods makes it easier for LPs to track progress over time and reduces back-and-forth from LP queries. Follow these sections in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Executive Summary">
    Open with a brief narrative covering fund highlights for the quarter, key performance metrics (TVPI, DPI, and net IRR), and the most notable portfolio events. Keep this section to one page. It is the section most LPs read first — and sometimes only — so make it accurate and substantive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund Performance">
    Present the full performance table showing cumulative capital calls, cumulative distributions, current NAV, TVPI, DPI, and net IRR — all clearly labeled with the as-of date and on a net basis. Include prior-period comparisons where available to show trajectory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Portfolio Overview">
    Provide a summary table of all active portfolio companies. Include company name, sector, investment stage, Capria's ownership percentage, cost basis, and current carrying value. This gives LPs a snapshot of the full portfolio in a single view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Company Updates">
    Write a brief update of 2–4 sentences for each active portfolio company. Cover business progress, key milestones reached, challenges, and any significant events (new funding rounds, leadership changes, product launches). Be candid — LPs value honesty over marketing language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capital Account Statement">
    Include each LP's individual capital account statement showing their cumulative contributions to the fund, cumulative distributions received, their allocated share of the current NAV, and their resulting net account balance. These statements must be reconciled with the fund administrator before distribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New Investments">
    Detail every investment made during the quarter. Include company name, investment date, instrument (equity, SAFE, convertible note), amount invested, post-money valuation, and a brief description of the business and investment thesis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exits and Realizations">
    Detail every full or partial exit during the quarter. Include company name, exit date, proceeds received, cost basis, gross MOIC on the position, and a brief description of the exit event (M&A, secondary sale, IPO, write-off).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Annual Report Additional Sections

The annual report includes everything in the quarterly report, plus the following additional sections that are required for full-year disclosure:

- **Audited financial statements** — Balance sheet, income statement, statement of changes in partners' capital, and statement of cash flows, prepared by an independent auditor and accompanied by the audit opinion letter
- **GP management letter** — A candid letter from the general partner covering the fund's strategic outlook, portfolio developments, market commentary, and any updates to the investment strategy or team
- **Valuation methodology disclosure** — A clear description of the valuation approach used for each portfolio company category (e.g., last-round pricing, comparable company analysis, discounted cash flow), in compliance with ASC 820 fair value standards
- **Fees and expenses summary** — A complete breakdown of management fees charged, fund expenses incurred, and carried interest accrued or paid during the year
- **Conflicts of interest disclosure** — Any material conflicts of interest involving the GP, its affiliates, or investment professionals, as required under the LPA

## Report Timeline

<Note>
  Capria targets the following standard LP reporting timeline. Build these deadlines into your calendar at the start of each year and coordinate with the fund administrator well in advance to ensure data is available on time.

  - **Quarterly reports** — Due 45–60 days after quarter-end (e.g., Q3 report due by November 15)
  - **Annual reports (unaudited)** — Due 90 days after year-end (March 31)
  - **Annual reports (audited)** — Due 120–180 days after year-end (April 30 – June 30)
  - **Capital call notices** — Issued 10–15 business days before the capital call due date
  - **Distribution notices** — Issued 5–10 business days before the distribution date
</Note>

## LP Portal Distribution

All LP reports and notices are distributed through Capria's LP portal, which provides each limited partner with a secure, individual login and access only to documents relevant to their fund participation. Follow this process for every distribution:

1. Upload the final, approved report to the LP portal and verify that it is correctly assigned to the relevant fund and LP accounts.
2. Send an email notification to all relevant LPs with the portal link, the report type, and the reporting period. Do not attach the full report to the email — direct LPs to the portal to ensure access controls and document versioning are maintained.
3. For capital call notices and distribution notices, follow up with a direct email to each LP (or their designated contact) to confirm receipt and provide wire instructions where applicable.

<Tip>
  Store a copy of every report distributed in the fund's document archive, recording the exact date sent and the complete list of recipients. This record is essential for demonstrating LPA compliance, responding to LP audit requests, and resolving any disputes about whether a notice was properly delivered.
</Tip>

## Data Quality Checklist

Before distributing any LP report, run through the pre-distribution checklist below. A single data error in an LP report — especially in capital account statements or wire instructions — can damage trust and create legal exposure.

<Accordion title="Pre-Distribution Checklist">
  Review every item below before approving a report for distribution. All items must be confirmed before the report is sent.

  - [ ] As-of date is correctly labeled on all performance data, portfolio tables, and capital account statements
  - [ ] TVPI, DPI, and IRR are reported on a **net basis** (after management fees, fund expenses, and carried interest)
  - [ ] Capital account statements are fully reconciled with the fund administrator and match the administrator's records exactly
  - [ ] All portfolio company valuations are current, reflect the most recent valuation event or quarterly mark, and have been reviewed and approved by the relevant team member
  - [ ] New investments made during the period are accurately reflected in the portfolio table, company updates, and new investments section
  - [ ] Exits and partial realizations are accurately reflected in fund performance metrics and the exits section
  - [ ] Wire instructions (for capital calls) have been verified against the fund's banking records — never copy wire details from a prior notice without re-verification
  - [ ] The report has been reviewed by at least two team members before final approval
</Accordion>
