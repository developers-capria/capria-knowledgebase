This page explains how to get help with the Capria knowledge base — whether you have a question about where to find information, need to report a data error, or want to request a new section or update. Reach out to the right contact for your question type to ensure issues are resolved quickly and by the person with the right authority to act.

## Who to Contact

Route your question to the correct contact the first time to avoid delays. Use the table below to identify who owns each type of question or issue.

| Question Type | Contact | How to Reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge base navigation or content questions | Knowledge base administrator | Email or team messaging |
| Fund financial data corrections (capital calls, distributions, NAV) | CFO | Direct email |
| Portfolio company data updates (profile, metrics, valuation) | Portfolio management team | Email or team messaging |
| LP reporting questions | Investor relations | Email |
| Legal or document questions | General Counsel | Email |
| Technical access issues (login, permissions, portal) | IT / Operations | Helpdesk ticket |

## Reporting a Data Error

Report data errors promptly — inaccurate data in the knowledge base can flow through to LP reports, investment committee materials, and fund performance calculations. Follow these steps to get a correction processed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the error">
    Note the specific page and field where the error appears, along with the current (incorrect) value. Be as specific as possible — for example: "Portfolio company profile for \[Company X\], 'Last Round Valuation' field shows \$12M; correct value is \$15M."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather the correct value and its source">
    Confirm the correct value and identify its authoritative source — for example, a cap table document, a fund administrator statement, a signed term sheet, or a board-approved valuation report. Do not submit a correction without a source document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the relevant data owner">
    Reach out to the appropriate contact from the table above based on the type of data. Include the page, the incorrect value, the correct value, and the source documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide supporting documentation">
    Attach or share the source document that supports the correction. This creates an audit trail and allows the data owner to verify the change independently before applying it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the correction is applied">
    The data owner will update the knowledge base and log the correction with a date and source reference. Confirm that the updated value is reflected correctly. If the error appeared in a previously distributed LP report, the data owner will assess whether a correction notice to LPs is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requesting New Content

The knowledge base evolves as the firm grows, new funds are launched, and processes change. To request a new section, page, or significant update to existing content, submit a content request to the knowledge base administrator with the following information:

- **Topic or section needed** — a clear description of what content is missing or needs to be created
- **Why it's needed** — the specific use case, team need, or gap in current documentation that the new content would address
- **Any existing source material or draft content** — relevant documents, process notes, templates, or draft text that can inform the new page

<Note>
  Content requests are reviewed monthly and prioritized based on team impact — requests that address high-frequency questions or fill critical process gaps will be scheduled first. Submit your request to the knowledge base administrator via Slack or email; you will receive a response with an estimated timeline.
</Note>

## Providing Feedback

Continuous feedback from the team is how the knowledge base stays accurate, complete, and useful. You do not need to wait for something to be wrong to provide feedback — improvements to clarity, structure, and completeness are always welcome.

Areas where feedback is especially valuable:

- **Unclear definitions** — if a term or concept is defined in a way that is ambiguous or inconsistent with how it is used in practice, flag it
- **Missing workflow steps** — if a process page omits a step that is actually required, suggest the addition with the specific step and where it belongs
- **Outdated content** — if a page describes a policy, template, or tool that has since changed, let the knowledge base administrator know with details about what has changed and when

<Tip>
  The most useful feedback includes a **specific suggestion**, not just a note that something is wrong. For example: "Step 3 on the capital call page should mention checking the LP side letter for modified notice periods" is far more actionable than "this page is incomplete." The clearer your suggestion, the faster it can be implemented.
</Tip>
