## Mandate

The fund was raised to take concentrated positions in roughly ten market leaders selected from the existing India portfolio — companies with established product-market fit, sound unit economics and proven leadership, at the stage where the earlier seed funds could no longer maintain their pro rata. Named targets at launch included BetterPlace, Masai School, Cuemath, Awign, 5C Network and Blowhorn.

Capria's argument for the structure was that an opportunity fund gives LPs exposure to the winners of a seed portfolio without the seed-stage loss rate, on a shorter fund life. The portfolio also includes one non-convertible debenture position.

## Structure

The master fund is a scheme of Unitus Seed Fund India II — the same Indian trust that houses the India Fund II master — registered with SEBI as a Category II AIF. Its private placement memorandum is dated 25 January 2023, and the Delaware feeder's limited partnership agreement took effect on 9 February 2023. Capria Ventures LLC is the investment manager under an agreement with UVOF I GP LLC as general partner.
