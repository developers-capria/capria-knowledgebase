## Mandate

The theme is **The Future of Work**, expressed as frontier technology applied to four sectors:

In **enterprise**, vertical AI for core business problems and AI-led transformation of supply chain, go-to-market and other critical functions. In **manufacturing**, automation and productivity including physical AI and robotics, IP-led manufacturing for import substitution, and B2B managed marketplaces. In **healthtech**, clinical excellence through technology, pursued in collaboration with anchor investor Claypond Capital. **Agritech** is opportunistic — deals of national importance sourced through venture partner Sanjiv Rangrass.

Underlying the thesis is India's MSME base: roughly 70 million enterprises driving 40% of GDP, which Capria treats as the engine of the next phase of growth and the primary customer for these technologies.

The fund is explicit about what it does not back: simple wrappers on existing technology, consumer tech, pure infrastructure plays and pure business-model innovation. What it looks for is proprietary technology with defensible moats in IP or data, and capital efficiency.

## Portfolio construction

Around 40–45% of capital goes into new Seed and pre-Series A tickets, with 50–55% held as follow-on reserves — deployed into Fund III companies and, opportunistically, into companies from the earlier India funds, with up to 30% of the fund available for those late-stage opportunities in predecessor portfolios to give an earlier return of capital. Exits for the later-stage follow-on positions are expected from the fourth year after first close; exits for new early-stage investments from the sixth.

## Structure

A Category II India AIF master fund with a Delaware LP feeder, the pattern established by Fund II. Fee structure is 2% per year with 20% carried interest over an 8% hurdle with full catch-up; the sponsor and GP commitment is INR 21.5 Cr (USD 2.5M), or 2.5% of target fund size.
