The successor to Global South Fund I, raised around a single idea: applied generative AI for the five billion people of the Global South. Fund II dropped most of the fund-of-funds component of its predecessor to concentrate on direct investments at Series A and A+.

## Mandate

Fund II targets the key tech hubs of its four regions — with an outsized focus on Bangalore, Mumbai, Mexico City, São Paulo, Jakarta, Singapore, Nairobi, Cairo and Lagos — at Series A and A+ valuations. The strong preference is for founders applying generative AI to build leapfrog businesses, in B2B or B2B2C models rather than consumer, with product-market fit already demonstrated.

Alongside the direct portfolio the fund holds a small pocket of venture fund commitments — Valor Fund IV, India Quotient IV, Integra II and NXTP Fund III — a much narrower fund-of-funds allocation than Fund I carried.

The fund was originally marketed at a \$50M target with 20–25 companies; it closed materially smaller, and as of the most recent LP materials has roughly one remaining ticket to deploy.

<Note>
  Because the marketed target and the final fund size differ, check which figure a document is quoting before reusing it. The \$25M above is the committed capital recorded in the ops sheet; the \$50M in older data-room documents was the fundraising target.
</Note>

## Structure

A straightforward Delaware limited partnership — no series structure, no India AIF layer — with Capria II GP LLC as general partner and Capria Ventures LLC as investment manager.
