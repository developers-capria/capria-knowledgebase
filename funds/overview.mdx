## Fund Lifecycle

Every venture capital fund follows a structured lifecycle — typically spanning ten years — divided into two distinct phases. Understanding where your fund sits in this lifecycle determines how you deploy capital, report performance, and manage LP expectations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Investment Period (Years 1–5)">
    During the investment period, you actively deploy committed capital into new portfolio companies. This is when the majority of capital calls occur — funding new investments, paying management fees, and covering fund expenses. Most fund documents restrict new investments after the investment period ends unless the investment committee approves an exception.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Harvest Period (Years 6–10)">
    During the harvest period, you shift focus from deploying capital to supporting existing portfolio companies and pursuing liquidity events. Follow-on investments in existing portfolio companies may still be permitted, but no new platform investments are made. Distributions to LPs typically begin accelerating during this phase as exits occur.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Fund Data

Keeping fund-level data accurate and up to date is the foundation of reliable performance reporting. The table below summarizes the most important data points tracked at the fund level, what each one means, and where to find it in the Capria knowledge base.

| Data Point | Description | Where to Find It |
|---|---|---|
| Committed Capital | Total capital pledged by all LPs at final close | Fund Summary → Capital Overview |
| Called Capital | Cumulative capital drawn down from LPs to date | Capital Calls → Running Totals |
| Uncalled Capital | Committed capital not yet drawn (dry powder) | Capital Calls → Remaining Commitments |
| NAV | Current net asset value of the fund's portfolio | Reporting → Fund Performance |
| Fund Vintage | Year of the fund's first capital call or first close | Fund Summary → Fund Details |
| Investment Period End Date | Date after which no new platform investments may be made | Fund Summary → Fund Details |
