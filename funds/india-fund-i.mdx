## Mandate

Fund I was Capria's pilot fund in an early ecosystem: a seed vehicle written when Bangalore had a handful of venture firms and almost no institutional seed capital. The thesis was expanding access to opportunity for Bharat — backing founders building for the roughly 750 million Indians underserved by the formal economy, across jobs, education, healthcare and financial access.

The fund is fully deployed and in its harvest phase. Positions in BetterPlace, Cuemath and DriveU carry the bulk of the remaining value, and two portfolio companies are on a path to public listing in 2027.

## Structure

Unlike every later India vintage, Fund I ran as two **parallel funds** rather than a master-feeder pair. The India-domiciled AIF (operating in INR) and the Cayman LP (operating in USD) each invested directly into portfolio companies, with the Cayman fund making two additional small investments the AIF did not. Both are managed by the same GP entity, USF GP LLC. Performance is reported separately for each vehicle, which is why Investment Records lists "Capria India Fund I" and "Capria India Fund I Cayman" as two rows.
