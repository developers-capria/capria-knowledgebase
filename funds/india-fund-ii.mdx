The second India vintage, raised in 2018 and fully deployed by March 2023. Where Fund I was broad, Fund II narrowed the thesis: Capria became a jobtech specialist for Bharat, backing platforms that create income and employment for India's informal and semi-formal workforce.

## Mandate

Fund II concentrated on jobtech and adjacent sectors serving Bharat: workforce platforms, skilling and education, and the financial products that sit alongside them. The portfolio is anchored by Masai School, BetterPlace, Eduvanz, New Street Tech and the Awign position, which was realized through a \$100M acquisition by Mynavi in 2023.

The fund is fully deployed and in its harvest phase. It is the vintage against which Capria benchmarks its India track record, and it also supplies much of the follow-on pipeline for the [India Opportunity Fund](/funds/india-opportunity-fund).

## Structure

Fund II introduced the **master-feeder structure** that every later India vintage uses. US and other offshore LPs commit to Unitus Seed Fund II, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, which invests solely into the India-domiciled master fund; the master fund makes the actual portfolio investments. Because the feeder holds nothing but master-fund units, portfolio holdings are reported at the master level.
