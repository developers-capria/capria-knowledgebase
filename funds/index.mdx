Capria has raised seven funds across two families since 2012, plus an SPV platform used to syndicate follow-on capital. This section is the context layer: what each fund was raised to do, where it invests, when it started and where it sits in its lifecycle. For capital deployed, realizations and returns, see [Investment Records](/portfolio/investments); for the legal entities behind each vehicle, see [Fund Structure](/funds/fund-structure).

<Note>
  These pages describe mandate and strategy only. They deliberately carry no performance figures — those live on the reporting pages and in the quarterly investor reports, which are the authoritative source.
</Note>

## The two families

The **India Funds Family** is Capria's original line, running from the 2012 seed-stage Unitus Seed Fund vehicles through to the current India Fund III. Every vintage pairs an India-domiciled AIF with a US or offshore vehicle that LPs commit to.

The **Global South Funds Family** invests across all four of Capria's regions — India, Latin America, Africa and Southeast Asia — out of a single US vehicle per vintage, with no India AIF layer. Capria LATAM Fund I, currently being raised, narrows that mandate to one region for the first time.

## How the mandates evolved

The India line has moved up the technology stack with each vintage. Fund I set out to expand access to opportunity for Bharat, Fund II narrowed into jobtech, the Opportunity Fund concentrated capital into the best of those two portfolios, and Fund III now backs frontier technology applied to enterprise, manufacturing and healthtech under a "Future of Work" theme.

The Global South line started as a hybrid: Fund I combined direct company investments with LP commitments into emerging-market managers, giving Capria both returns and a sourcing network. Fund II dropped most of the fund-of-funds component to concentrate on applied GenAI companies at Series A. LATAM Fund I takes the regional expertise built through both and commits it to a single geography.
