Capria's first single-region fund outside India, currently being raised. It narrows the Global South mandate to Latin America and to one lens: applied AI as the source of value creation.

<Warning>
  LATAM Fund I has not yet held its first close. The fund entities are still to be formed, terms are drafts, and the source documents are confidential and marked as such. Treat everything on this page as subject to change and do not circulate figures externally without checking with the LATAM team.
</Warning>

## Mandate

The objective is market-rate, long-term capital appreciation from high-growth, tech-enabled businesses operating primarily in Latin America, with Capria's applied-AI expertise as the value-creation lever.

Capital is split across two stages. Roughly 60% goes to companies raising Series B or equivalent with proven traction, on the rationale that AI integration can accelerate companies that already have momentum. The remaining 40% goes to Series A companies that are AI-native or all-in on AI and positioned to become regional category leaders that can go global. Both labels are used as indicators of maturity rather than strict round names.

Three verticals carry the thesis. **Fintech** — stablecoin-based cross-border payments, SME financing, and B2B payments infrastructure — remains the dominant wedge for digitization in the region. **B2B SaaS**, where AI-native sales intelligence moves from nice-to-have tooling to strategic revenue driver. And **AI for the backbone economy**: bringing AI to the factory floor, the warehouse and the traditional offline trades still operating on paper, a particular opportunity in Mexico where nearshoring is accelerating legacy-industry digitization.

The fund is positioned as an escalation of an existing presence rather than a market entry: Capria has made 11 direct LatAm investments through partnerships with nine LatAm funds, and monitors 190+ underlying portfolio companies through its Global South fund commitments.

## Fundraising approach

Capria is prioritizing traction and commitments from LATAM-based LPs before offering allocation to US investors, on the view that the foundational capital should come from within the region to keep the fund aligned with the local ecosystem.

## Terms

Management fee averages 2% over the first ten years, stepping from 2.5% in years 1–4 to 2.0% in years 5–8 and 1.0% from year 9. Carried interest is 20% over a 6% preferred return with full GP catch-up. Capria-associated persons commit at least 1% of total fund commitments. Portfolio concentration is capped at 20% in any one investment and 30% in companies backed by other Capria funds.
