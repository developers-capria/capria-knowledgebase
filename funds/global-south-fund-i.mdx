Capria's first multi-region fund and the vehicle that built its Global South network. Fund I is a hybrid: it invests directly into companies and, at the same time, takes LP positions in emerging-market venture managers, which gives Capria both a return stream and a proprietary view of deal flow across four regions.

## Mandate

Fund I was built to reach the tech hubs of the Global South at a point when few Western LPs had access to them: Bangalore and Mumbai, Mexico City and São Paulo, Jakarta and Singapore, Nairobi, Cairo and Lagos. Rather than choosing between backing managers and backing companies, it did both — committing to roughly 15 emerging-market funds while making its own direct investments alongside them, and extending GP loans where a manager needed working capital to reach a close.

Direct positions initiated by Fund I span fintech, agtech and food, jobtech and HR tech, healthtech, edtech and mobility, including Moniepoint (formerly TeamApt), Helium Health, Alami, EdenFarm, BetterPlace, BharatAgri, Eduvanz, Agrofy and MAX.

Because the fund-of-funds component matures on a longer cycle than direct investing, distributions develop later in the fund's life than they would in a pure direct vehicle.

## Structure

Capria Fund LLC is a **Delaware Series LLC**: each investment sits in its own named series — Seed Series, LatAm Series, Africa Series, Asia Series, and per-investment series such as Lateral, Vakayi, BetterPlace, BharatAgri and TeamApt PSW. There is no India AIF layer, since the mandate is not India-specific. Capria Manager LLC, a Washington LLC, is the GP.
