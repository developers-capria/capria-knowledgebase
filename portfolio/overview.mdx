## Keeping this current

The tables here are rebuilt from the quarterly holdings extract. Three systems sit behind every company and fund manager page, and each has one job:

| System | What lives there |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Drive** | Deal memos, IC materials, board decks, quarterly reporting |
| **Dropbox** | Executed agreements, diligence packs, legal and compliance files, under `Investments/<Region>/Portfolio - Co` and `Portfolio - FM` |
| **Attio** | The live CRM record — founders, contacts, interactions, co-investors, sector and stage tags |

<Note>
  When a company's details change, update Attio first. Company pages here mirror the Attio record, so an out-of-date CRM shows up everywhere else.
</Note>
