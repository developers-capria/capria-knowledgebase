## Reading these numbers

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Committed | Capital Capria has contractually committed to a fund manager, drawn and undrawn |
| Invested capital | Capital actually deployed into the position |
| Realizations | Cash and marketable proceeds returned from the position |
| Fair value | Carrying value of the remaining position at the valuation date |
| Total value | Realizations plus fair value |
| Gross MOIC | Total value divided by invested capital, before fees, expenses and carry |

<Note>
  A few positions carry a small negative fair value at the reporting date from valuation adjustments and rounding; they are shown as \$0 in summary tables.
</Note>
