## What sits on each company page

Every company page carries the same four blocks:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Links out">
    Google Drive folder, Dropbox folder and Attio record — the three places a company's information actually lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position summary">
    Status, region, which funds hold it, first investment date, invested capital, realizations, fair value and gross MOIC.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Positions by fund">
    One row per fund or vehicle holding the company, so cross-fund positions like BetterPlace and Eduvanz are legible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile, contacts and co-investors">
    Sector, headquarters, founding year, team size, website and revenue band from Attio, plus the Capria owner, the primary contact and the rest of the cap table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Folder conventions

Company files follow a fixed structure in both systems:

| System | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Dropbox | `Investments / <Region> / Portfolio - Co / <Company>` |
| Dropbox (warehoused) | `Investments / <Region> / Portfolio - FM / <Manager> / Warehouse / Warehouse - <Company>` |
| Dropbox (exited India) | `Investments / India / Portfolio - Co / x-Exited / <Company>` |
| Google Drive | `<Region> Portfolio - Co / <Company>` |

<Note>
  A handful of older positions have no dedicated folder yet. Their Drive and Dropbox links open the regional parent folder instead, and the card on the company page says so.
</Note>
