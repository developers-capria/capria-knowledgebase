## Company Highlights

### Wiffy — 1.7x _(Only marked-up position)_

Full-stack B2B and B2C post-purchase home furnishing platform — SaaS for brands, consumer-facing service tracking, and technician upskilling.

- **ARR:** INR 85 Cr (\+30% YoY)
- **Gross margin:** 27%; EBITDA positive for 10\+ consecutive months
- **Scale:** 1M\+ homes served to date; June loose furniture installations \+22% vs target
- **Series A closed May 2026:** Pre-money INR 250 Cr → Post-money INR 264 Cr (round size: INR 14 Cr). India III invested pro-rata (INR 1.6 Cr).

### Cusmat — Profitable outlier at seed stage

Two products: **SkillOps** (AR/VR simulation for industrial and technical workforces) and **AutoTechs AI** (OEM repair manuals converted to 3D AI-guided workflows for auto technicians).

- **FY26 revenue:** INR 6.36 Cr (~USD 0.7M), \+60% YoY
- **Gross margin:** 80.7% (software-grade)
- **EBITDA:** Positive in FY26 at ~4% margin
- Profitable vs peers who have raised significantly more and remain loss-making.

### ThirdAI — Largest ownership position (9.33%)

AI automation for manufacturing and industrial workflows. Invested Feb 2026 — too early for a marked-up valuation. Largest equity stake in the portfolio.

### FloWorks — Largest capital deployed (₹12.60 Cr)

Conversational AI interface (Flowy agent) automating sales and workflow tasks. Held as CCD; conversion mechanics tied to future pricing round.
